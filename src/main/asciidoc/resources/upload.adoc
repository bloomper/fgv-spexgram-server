Photos and videos are uploaded with https://tus.io/protocols/resumable-upload[tus 1.0.0], with the extensions `creation` and
`termination`, so a large file survives a broken connection.
The upload's id is then given to a GraphQL mutation that sends the file to Immich: `itemsAdd` for editors, `contributionsAdd` for members, or `itemReplaceFile`.
The metadata may give `filename`, and `lastModified`: the file's last change on the uploader's device in milliseconds since 1970, which Immich falls back to for photos without a date taken.
Editors upload to any album; members' uploads count against their daily contribution limit (`Limits`).
An upload that is not used is deleted after 24 hours.
Only its owner can reach an upload.

Send chunks of at most 50 MB: Cloudflare, in front of staging, refuses larger requests.
